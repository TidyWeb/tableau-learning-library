Tableau is a chain of decisions

Tableau is not just a collection of chart buttons. It lets you connect data, preserve the meaning of each table, ask a visual question, and let a reader explore the answer.

Working principle: every visual should answer a question, every filter should have a purpose, and every total should survive a grain and relationship check.

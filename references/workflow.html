The Tableau operating model

Use this page when you lose track of which Tableau area you are in or what should happen next.

Four layers

From rows to a story

LayerObjectWhat changesTypical mistake
1Tables and relationshipsHow records connect and what one row means.Joining tables with different grains and inflating totals.
2Fields and calculationsTypes, missing values, dimensions, measures and business rules.Using a string as a number or hiding nulls without understanding them.
3Worksheet viewFields become shelves, marks, axes, labels and filters.Adding fields until the view is busy without a question.
4Dashboard/storyViews become an interactive surface or a narrative sequence.Publishing before checking privacy, filters and display size.

Field placement

A shelf is an instruction

Columns

Defines horizontal comparison or the x-axis.

Rows

Defines vertical comparison or the y-axis.

Marks card

Controls colour, size, label, detail and shape.

Filters

Restricts the records or values included in the view.

Decision loop

Ask, build, inspect, revise

  1. State the question in one sentence.
  2. Choose the grain and fields that answer it.
  3. Build the smallest useful view.
  4. Inspect totals, filters, nulls, dates and marks.
  5. Only then add colour, actions, dashboards or story points.
Professional habit: save a known baseline before a major change. If the result changes, you can explain whether the cause was the data, the relationship, the filter, the aggregation or the visual design.
